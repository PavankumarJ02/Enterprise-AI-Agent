## 1. Scope & Service Level Commitments
This agreement defines the technical support tiers, guaranteed response times, operational availability windows, and service credits provided to Enterprise AI Agent customers. All commitments are legally binding pursuant to the Enterprise Master Services Agreement (MSA).

## 4. Service Uptime Commitments & Service Credit Schedules
The platform guarantees monthly system availability excluding scheduled maintenance windows (which occur Sundays 02:00 - 04:00 UTC with 7 calendar days advance notice). If the monthly uptime percentage drops below contracted commitments, customers are entitled to financial service credits:

### 4.1 Enterprise Tier (99.99% Target) Credit Table
- **99.90% to < 99.99%:** **10%** of monthly contract subscription fee credited.
- **99.00% to < 99.90%:** **25%** of monthly contract subscription fee credited.
- **95.00% to < 99.00%:** **50%** of monthly contract subscription fee credited.
- **< 95.00%:** **100%** of monthly contract subscription fee credited + option for early contract termination for cause.

### 4.2 Claim Procedure
Customers must submit a formal SLA credit request to their Customer Success Manager within **30 calendar days** following the end of the affected calendar month. Credits are applied against the next recurring billing invoice.
