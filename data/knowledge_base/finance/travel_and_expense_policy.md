# Corporate Travel, Entertainment & Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-2026-002  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Global Employees, Managers, and Corporate Officers  
**Department:** Corporate Finance, Accounting & Accounts Payable  

---

## 1. Objective & General Principles
The Enterprise reimburses employees for reasonable, necessary, and authorized business expenses incurred while conducting company business, traveling on commercial assignments, or entertaining prospective clients. Employees are expected to exercise fiscal prudence and treat company funds with the same care as their personal finances.

---

## 2. Daily Meal Per Diems & Dining Guidelines

### 2.1 Per Diem Allowances
Employees traveling overnight on approved corporate business are granted fixed daily meal per diem allowances:
- **Domestic Travel (US & Canada):** **$75 USD per day**
  - Breakfast: $15 | Lunch: $25 | Dinner: $35
- **International Travel:** **$100 USD per day**
  - Breakfast: $20 | Lunch: $30 | Dinner: $50
- **High-Cost Tier 1 Metros (London, Tokyo, Zurich, San Francisco, NYC):** Add **$25 USD surcharge per day** ($100 domestic / $125 international).

### 2.2 Client Entertainment & Team Dinners
- Team meals (internal gatherings) require Director pre-approval and are capped at **$60 USD per person** inclusive of tax and gratuity.
- Client entertainment dinners require Vice President pre-approval and must explicitly record business purpose and attendee names on expense receipts. Alcohol must remain incidental (< 30% of total bill).

---

## 3. Commercial Air Travel & Ground Transportation

### 3.1 Flight Booking Class Standards
- **Domestic Flights (< 6 hours nonstop):** Economy Class / Main Cabin only. Preferred seating upgrades are permitted for flights over 4 hours.
- **Transcontinental & International Flights (>= 6 hours nonstop):** Premium Economy is standard.
- **Executive / Ultra-Long-Haul (> 10 hours continuous flight):** Business Class is authorized for VP level and above, or upon explicit written authorization from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
- **First Class:** Prohibited under all circumstances.

### 3.2 Ground Transportation
- Ride-sharing (Uber, Lyft) standard tier (UberX, Lyft Standard) should be used. Luxury tiers (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) are non-reimbursable.
- Rental cars are authorized only when public transit or ride-share options are impractical or cost-prohibitive. Compact or Intermediate vehicle classes must be selected.

---

## 4. Lodging & Hotel Accommodations
- **Tier 1 Metropolitan Cities:** Standard room rates are capped at **$250 USD per night** (excluding local occupancy taxes).
- **Tier 2 / Regional Locations:** Room rates are capped at **$180 USD per night**.
- Ineligible hotel charges: In-room pay-per-view movies, spa treatments, mini-bar alcohol snacks, and personal laundry services (unless travel duration exceeds 5 consecutive days).

---

## 5. Expense Submission Deadlines & Documentation Requirements
- **Receipt Threshold:** Itemized receipts are legally mandatory for any single transaction exceeding **$25.00 USD**. Credit card statements without itemized vendor receipts will be rejected by Accounts Payable.
- **Submission Deadline:** All expense reports must be submitted via the corporate expense portal within **30 calendar days** from the conclusion of travel. Expenses submitted beyond 60 days require CFO exception approval.
- **Corporate Credit Cards:** Balances on company-issued cards must be reconciled monthly. Unexplained or personal charges will be deducted from payroll.
