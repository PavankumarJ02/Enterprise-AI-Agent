# Global Employee Benefits, Wellness & Parental Leave Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2026-001  
**Version:** 3.4  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Enterprise Employees  
**Department:** Global People Operations & Human Resources  

---

## 1. Overview & Philosophy
The Enterprise is dedicated to supporting the holistic wellbeing, lifelong learning, and family life of all team members. This policy outlines our comprehensive benefits packages, annual professional development stipends, healthcare reimbursement schemes, and parental leave entitlements across all global entities.

---

## 2. Annual Learning & Professional Development Stipend
Continuous professional development directly empowers organizational excellence and innovation.

### 2.1 Funding Allocation & Eligibility
- **Annual Allowance:** All full-time employees are allocated an annual learning budget of **$1,200 USD** per calendar year (pro-rated for mid-year hires).
- **Eligible Expenses:**
  - Technical and leadership books, e-books, and audiobooks.
  - Accredited certification exam fees (e.g., AWS, GCP, CISA, CISSP, PMP).
  - Virtual and in-person industry conference tickets.
  - Subscriptions to approved technical learning platforms (e.g., Coursera, O'Reilly Learning, Udemy Business).
- **Ineligible Expenses:** General university tuition towards degree programs (covered under separate Academic Sponsorship Policy), hardware or electronics, and non-work-related hobby courses.

### 2.2 Reimbursement Procedure
Employees must submit receipts via the Workday Expense portal under the category **"Professional Development"** within 60 calendar days of purchase. Manager pre-approval is required for single purchases exceeding $300 USD.

---

## 3. Health & Wellness Reimbursement
Physical and mental wellbeing are fundamental to sustainable high performance.

### 3.1 Wellness Allowance
- **Monthly Allocation:** **$50 USD per month** ($600 USD annually).
- **Approved Categories:** Gym memberships, fitness studio classes (e.g., yoga, pilates), meditation app subscriptions (e.g., Headspace, Calm), ergonomic home accessories, and competitive race entry fees (e.g., 10K, half-marathon).
- **Mental Health Support:** In addition to the monetary allowance, all employees and their immediate household dependents have access to **12 free confidential therapy sessions per year** through the Enterprise Employee Assistance Program (EAP) powered by Modern Health.

---

## 4. Parental Leave Entitlements
The Enterprise provides inclusive, generous leave for new parents, recognizing the vital importance of bonding time during early childhood transitions.

### 4.1 Paid Parental Leave Duration
- **Primary and Secondary Caregivers:** All eligible employees receive **16 weeks of 100% fully paid parental leave**.
- **Qualifying Events:**
  - Birth of a child (biological mother or partner).
  - Placement of a child for legal adoption.
  - Long-term foster care placement of a minor child.
- **Service Requirement:** Employees become eligible after completing **6 months of continuous active service** prior to the qualifying event.

### 4.2 Leave Structure & Flexibility
- Parental leave must be taken within **12 months** following the birth or placement date.
- Leave may be taken as a single continuous 16-week block, or in two blocks with a minimum duration of 4 weeks per block, subject to operational coordination with department leadership.
- Health insurance, 401(k) / pension matching, and equity vesting continue without interruption during the 16-week paid leave window.

---

## 5. Bereavement, Compassionate & Caregiver Leave
- **Immediate Family Bereavement:** Up to **10 consecutive paid business days** following the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- **Extended Family Bereavement:** Up to **5 consecutive paid business days** for grandparents, in-laws, or domestic partners.
- **Family Caregiver Leave:** Up to **4 weeks of paid caregiver leave** per rolling 24-month period to provide primary care to an immediate family member experiencing a certified critical illness.
