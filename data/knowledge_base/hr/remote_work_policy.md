# Enterprise Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-2026-002  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026  
**Applicability:** Global Knowledge Workers, Engineering, Operations, and Business Teams  
**Department:** Global Workplace Solutions & People Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
The Enterprise embraces a distributed-first workplace strategy that balances personal schedule flexibility with high-trust synchronous collaboration. This policy establishes guidelines for remote working arrangements, home office setup stipends, core synchronous collaboration hours, and digital security compliance.

---

## 2. Workplace Classification Models
Every role in the organization is designated under one of three workplace archetypes upon hire or mutual agreement:
1. **Remote-First:** Employees working primarily from an approved home office location with zero requirement for regular office attendance.
2. **Hybrid:** Employees working 2 to 3 days per week from an Enterprise regional office hub, with remaining days worked remotely.
3. **Office-Centric:** Roles requiring specialized on-premise hardware, physical lab equipment, or secure datacenter access.

---

## 3. Home Office Setup Stipend
To ensure safe, ergonomic, and productive home workspaces, the Enterprise provides financial support for equipment acquisition.

### 3.1 One-Time Setup Allowance
- **Grant Amount:** **$800 USD** provided to all newly hired remote and hybrid team members in their initial paycheck.
- **Eligible Items:**
  - Ergonomic office chairs (e.g., Herman Miller, Steelcase, ErgoChair).
  - Motorized standing desks or desk converters.
  - High-resolution external monitors (up to 4K / 34-inch ultrawide).
  - External webcams, noise-canceling headsets, and studio microphones.
  - Surge protectors, monitor arms, and ergonomic keyboard/mouse peripherals.
- **Ownership:** Equipment purchased with the stipend is considered employee-owned and does not require return upon voluntary or involuntary departure.

### 3.2 Monthly Internet & Connectivity Reimbursement
- Remote employees receive a monthly tax-free internet stipend of **$60 USD per month** to offset broadband internet costs.
- Minimum connectivity requirement: Consistent downstream bandwidth of at least **50 Mbps** and upstream bandwidth of at least **10 Mbps** with sub-50ms latency.

---

## 4. Core Synchronous Collaboration Hours
To protect deep work while maintaining cross-functional project momentum, all distributed teams observe standard core working hours.

### 4.1 Synchronous Windows
- **Core Collaboration Hours:** **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** local regional time (or aligned team timezone: EST, PST, or CET).
- **Expectations During Core Hours:**
  - Available on Slack with accurate status indicators (In a meeting, Lunch, Focused deep work).
  - Response time to direct mentions and high-priority alerts within 45 minutes.
  - Attendance at designated team standups, sprint reviews, and sprint retrospectives.
- **Focus Time / No-Meeting Windows:** Thursdays from 1:00 PM to 5:00 PM are designated as **"Deep Work Focus Blocks"** across all engineering and product teams—no internal meetings may be scheduled.

---

## 5. Information Security & Compliance Standards for Remote Work
Remote team members handle sensitive enterprise data and intellectual property. Compliance with the following controls is mandatory:
- **Corporate Hardware:** All production work must be performed exclusively on Enterprise-provisioned MacBook Pro or ThinkPad workstations managed via Jamf / Microsoft Intune.
- **VPN & Zero-Trust Access:** Direct access to internal VPCs, staging clusters, and telemetry dashboards requires active authentication through the Cloudflare Zero-Trust / WireGuard gateway.
- **Public Wi-Fi Prohibitions:** Employees may not connect to open, password-free public Wi-Fi networks without an active full-tunnel VPN session.
- **Screen Privacy:** When working in public spaces (e.g., cafes, airports, co-working lounges), physical privacy screen filters must be affixed to laptop screens when viewing customer data or confidential source code.

---

## 6. Temporary Cross-Border Remote Work (Workation)
Employees in good standing may request authorization to work remotely from an alternative country or international jurisdiction for up to **30 business days per rolling calendar year**, provided:
- Advance approval is obtained from their Director and the People Operations Tax Compliance team at least 4 weeks prior.
- The destination country is not subject to international trade sanctions or OFAC export controls.
- Time zone overlap includes at least 3 hours of core team collaboration time.
